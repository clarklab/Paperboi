PAPERBOI

a faithful Paperboy homage — and an experiment in how thin a game can be.

WHY THIS IS NOVEL

There is no engine, no framework, no library, and no assets — not one image, sound file, or sprite sheet was loaded to show you this. The whole game is one small C file compiled directly to WebAssembly with plain clang (--target=wasm32 -nostdlib, no emscripten, not even a C standard library).

The world isn't stored anywhere — it's math. Each frame, the renderer inverts the 2:1 isometric transform per pixel, so lawns, driveways, doormats, flowers, sidewalk joints and lane dashes are just arithmetic on world coordinates. Houses and cars are procedural boxes depth-sorted on the fly. Even the font is 15 bits per glyph.

JavaScript contains zero game logic: it blits the framebuffer, reads your finger, and beeps square waves when the wasm raises an event bit. The wasm module is embedded in this very page as text — view source: what you see is the entire game.
